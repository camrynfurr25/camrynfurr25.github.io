Story

You are a knight that was transported from your current timeline, back 200 years into a war that your great, great grandmother fought in. She has no knowledge of who you are but you must fight alongside her in order for your kingdom to win this war.

The Peaceful route

You have decided to go through the peaceful village. There you are able to join forces with allies, trade, and rest on your long journey. After three days you come upon the shoreline, you and your band of soldiers can either take a fairy to the island between the country you are currently in and rest, or continue through the mystic bog to your left.
