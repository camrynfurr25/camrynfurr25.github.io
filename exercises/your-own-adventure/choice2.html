Story

You are a knight that was transported from your current timeline, back 200 years into a war that your great, great grandmother fought in. She has no knowledge of who you are but you must fight alongside her in order for your kingdom to win this war.

The Treacherous Route

You have decided to avoid taking the long route and go through the dingy, old underground tunnels to save time. Along the way a forest sprite promises he knows the best rout to your kingdom. Do you trust him?

Yes, he seems friendly. No, this seems like a trap.
