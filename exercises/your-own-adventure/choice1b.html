Story

You are a knight that was transported from your current timeline, back 200 years into a war that your great, great grandmother fought in. She has no knowledge of who you are but you must fight alongside her in order for your kingdom to win this war.

The Mystic Bog Route

With many sighs of disapproval, your crew follows you through the mystic bog. They are upset about taking the longer route, though they follow you with loyalty anyways. After a week you make it safely to the wargrounds of your home country.
